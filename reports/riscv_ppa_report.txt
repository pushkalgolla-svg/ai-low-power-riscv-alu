[ENGINEERING ANALYSIS & ARCHITECTURAL VERDICT]
1. Dynamic Switching Activity Suppression:
   - Arithmetic datapath inputs achieved -54.4% transition toggle reduction (-1543 toggles).
   - Barrel shifter inputs achieved -52.1% transition toggle reduction (-775 toggles).
   - Logic datapath inputs achieved -18.1% transition toggle reduction (-515 toggles).
   - Operator-level boundary: In unshared architectures where distinct operators sit un-gated, total arithmetic
     operator transitions dropped by -84.8% due to folding into a single gated 33-bit adder.
   - Mechanism: Operand isolation (input gating) forces arithmetic, shifter, and logic inputs
     to zero when their respective execution blocks are inactive, preventing parasitic toggles
     through high-capacitance carry chains and shift matrices.
2. Resource Sharing Architecture:
   - Rather than instantiating separate operators for ADD, SUB, SLT, and SLTU, all four operations
     are computed using a single unified 33-bit two's-complement adder.
   - Subtraction, SLT, and SLTU invert operand B (b_comp = ~arith_b) and assert carry-in (cin = 1).
   - Signed comparison (SLT) evaluates sign bits and adder sign: (a[31] != b[31]) ? a[31] : add_sub_res[31].
   - Unsigned comparison (SLTU) evaluates adder carry-out: ~add_sub_res[32].
3. Netlist Interconnect & Area Trade-Off:
   - Netlist wires (nets) reduced by -18.4%.
   - Cell count changed by +0.9%: Input gating multiplexers add a nominal +0.9% gate
     overhead, which trades minimal static silicon area for >50% dynamic toggle suppression in major sub-blocks.
4. Timing Limitations & Target Technology Disclaimer:
   - Critical Path Delay: Input gating multiplexers introduce approximately one 2-to-1 MUX propagation delay
     on operand arrival. Because the arithmetic carry chain and multi-stage barrel shifter dominate the overall
     critical path, gating insertion has negligible impact on maximum clock frequency (Fmax).
   - Technology Disclaimer: The metrics above represent simulated RTL switching-activity estimates (signal bit
     transitions) and generic CMOS technology-independent netlist complexity (Yosys synth -noabc).
     They do NOT represent physical power in milliwatts (mW) or post-layout silicon area (um^2).
     Accurate physical power and static timing analysis (STA) require foundry standard-cell library models (.lib),
     wire parasitics (SPEF), and timing constraints (.sdc) via OpenSTA/OpenROAD.
