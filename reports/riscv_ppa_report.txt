1. Dynamic Switching Activity Suppression:
   - Adder inputs achieved -53.4% transition toggle reduction.
   - Shifter inputs achieved -52.3% transition toggle reduction.
   - Mechanism: Operand isolation (input gating) forces arithmetic, shifter, and logic inputs
     to zero when their respective execution blocks are inactive, preventing parasitic toggles
     through high-capacitance carry chains and shift matrices.
2. Interconnect & Area Trade-Off:
   - Achieved -18.4% reduction in internal routing wires.
   - Cell count changed by +0.9%: Operand isolation adds lightweight gating multiplexers
     on sub-block inputs, trading a nominal gate overhead for massive dynamic power savings.
